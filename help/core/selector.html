Selector View

The list view is used to show a list of selectable options. There may be unselectable headings dividing sections of the list. Selecting an entry may toggle a value, open the parameter editor or navigate to another view. Use knob 4 to browse the list and press to select the item. There may be alternative actions available with bold or long press. Use BACK to exit the list.

Each list entry may have some information describing the entry. This description may be sufficient to avoid the need for a dedicated help page, hence why you are seeing this generic page.
