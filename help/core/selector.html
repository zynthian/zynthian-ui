Selector View

The selector is a generic view used to show a list of selectable options. There may be unselectable headings dividing sections of the list.

Selecting an entry may navigate to another view, toggle an option or open the parameter editor. Rotate knob 4 to browse the list and press to select the item. There may be alternative actions available with bold press. Use BACK to exit.

Each list entry may have some information describing the entry. If the description does not fit the available space, it may be scrolled with knob 3. Pressing knob 3 may show more details if beneficial.
