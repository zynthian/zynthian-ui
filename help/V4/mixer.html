Mixer View

The mixer is the zynthian's root view. You can always access the mixer by pushing the MIX button. You can also push BACK until reaching it. From the launcher, you can access the mixer by pushing BACK. Similarly, from the mixer, you can access the launcher by pushing BACK. Mixer and Launcher are, indeed, a single view with 2 modes.

It's composed of vertical strips associated with chains. Initially it will be empty, showing the main strip alone. For each chain you create, a new strip is added to the mixer. There can be more mixer strips than can be displayed. They will scroll left / right if you select a strip that is off screen.

Each strip is an interactive widget that can be manipulated in several ways, showing status info about the associated chain. From top to bottom:

  • Rec Arm: Record-armed flag. Push to toggle. Audio output from record-armed chains is included in multi-track recordings as separated track. From admin menu, this can be configured as any other mixer toggle: solo, pfl, mono, phase or M+S. When configured as solo, it's non-exclusive solo mode. When a chain is solo-ed, all not solo-ed chains will be muted, allowing auditioning of one or more chains. The main solo will disable all chain solos.
  • Mute: Mute flag. Push to toggle. When a chain is muted it will remain silent.
  • Balance/Pan: Mixing right/left balance (or pan of a mono signal) for the associated chain. Rotate knob 2 or drag left & right on the fader to modify.
  • Fader: Mixing volume for the associated chain. Rotate knob 1 or drag up & down to modify the fader level. On the touchscreen you can drag multiple faders at once. Tap it to set the active chain.
  • DPM: Digital Peak Meter for the associated chain, covering the range from -50dBFS to 0dBFS.
  • Description: A vertically oriented text describing the chain. By default, for a synth chain it will show synth processor and preset name. If you give a name to the chain, this will be included in the description too.
  • Legend: It shows an icon for the chain's type and a number for the assigned MIDI channel. Tap it to access the chain's control view.

Adding chains

To create a new chain from the mixer view you can change to launcher view (short BACK) and short-push F3. Alternately, you can open the main menu (short OPT) and choose the Add Chain option.

Chain selection

The active chain is indicated by the legend at the bottom of the strip being highlighted red. You may change active chain by rotating Knob 4. A thin strip at the bottom of the legend indicates the chain is active for MIDI input.

Moving chains

You can reorganize your columns (chains), moving any chain horizontally to the position you want. You can also dock the chains you want into the right side, sticked to the main chain.

From the mixer view, F3 is not available for moving chains. Please, change to the launcher view (short BACK) for moving chains.
