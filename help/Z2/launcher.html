Launcher View

The launcher is the zynthian's sequencer main view. You can always access the launcher by pushing the PAD button. You can also push BACK repeatedly until reaching it.

From the mixer, you can access the launcher by pushing BACK. Similarly, from the launcher, you can access the mixer by pushing BACK. Launcher and Mixer are, indeed, a single view with 2 modes.

The launcher is a grid where columns match mixer's strips. Rows are called "phrases", being quite similar to Ableton Live's "scenes". Chain columns contain sequencer patterns or audio clips. The main column contains phrase launchers.

Initially the grid will be empty, showing the main strip alone. When adding a chain, a new column is added to the grid. When deleting a chain, the associated grid column will be removed. Note the associated patterns are not deleted. They are associated with the MIDI channel and if you create a new chain with the same MIDI channel, the removed patterns will be available again.

Short-push on any cell to play/stop the associated sequence, audio clip or launch a phrase.

Push STEP or Bold-push to edit the selected sequence or audio clip. If the cell contains a sequence, the pattern editor will be opened, if the cell contains an audio clip, the audio clip control view.

Push STEP or Bold-push on a phrase launcher (main column) to open the phrase options and configure the phrase parameters and manage phrases.

The phrase parameters are:

  • Duration: Number of bars. By default, it takes the value from the longer sequence in the row
  • Tempo: When the phrase is launched, the tempo is changed to the specified value in BPM.
  • Beats per Bar: The time signature used in patterns.
  • Automate: "Loop from", "loops to play" and "jump to" options allow to create simple song structures.

The phrase management options are:

  • Insert Phrase: Insert a new phrase (row) in the launcher.
  • Clone Phrase: Clone the selected phrase (row).
  • Move Phrase: Move the selected phrase (row) to a new position.
  • Delete Phrase: Delete the selected phrase (row).

When inserting, cloning, moving or deleting phrases, you may need to adjust automation parameteres of the affected phrases.

Chain selection

The active chain is indicated by the legend at the bottom of the strip being highlighted. You may change active chain by using the left/right arrow buttons or rotating knob 4. A thin strip at the bottom of the legend indicates the chain is active for MIDI input, not user interface actions. These may differ if for example the active chain is audio only.

Moving Chains

You can reorganize your columns (chains), moving any chain horizontally to the position you want. You can also dock the chains you want into the right side, sticked to the main chain:

  • Bold-push knob 3 to start moving the selected chain. The chain legend will display a double-arrow symbol.
  • Rotate knob 4 to move the chain to the desired position. To dock a chain into the right side, simply keep moving it to the right.
  • Push SELECT or BACK to finish.

Note you can also move your chains like this from the mixer's view.

Copy/paste patterns and audio clips

To copy/paste patterns or audio clips across the launcher cells you must enter ALT mode and use the buffer keys F1/F2 to copy/paste, like this:

  • Bold-push F1/F2 to copy the content of the selected cell into the buffer. The F1/F2 will blink in orange.
  • Rotate knob 3 & 4 to move the cursor to move the cursor to the desired position. Note the buffer button's blinking color changes when you move the cursor. It blinks in green when you can paste and blinks in red when you can't paste (i.e. you can't paste a sequencer pattern into an audio clip cell).
  • Short-push F1/F2 to paste. You can paste the buffer as many times as you want. If the destination cell is not empty, confirmation will be requested before overwriting.
